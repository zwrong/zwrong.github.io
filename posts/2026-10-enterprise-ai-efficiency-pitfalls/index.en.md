I want to share the pitfalls I hit while pushing AI efficiency at a company, so others don't have to hit them too. AI efficiency work in companies is still at a very early starting point — nowhere near the era of everyday AI chatbots like Doubao yet. And AI efficiency means the work doesn't directly make the company money, which is one of the big pain points. In other words, it can only cut costs; it doesn't seem able to bring in revenue. Of course, once people are freed from mechanical work, they can go take on more high-value tasks. Getting to that point still takes quite a lot of work — not just in communication and trust, but above all in resolve.

## Align With Your Boss on What AI Is

When we're doing AI efficiency work at a company, the first thing to get right is that we and our boss share the same understanding of what AI is. Getting there is genuinely hard, though. So what matters more is having the awareness ourselves — the "AI" we talk about and the "AI" our boss talks about may not be the same thing at all. Carry that awareness into every conversation with our boss and with the team: bring more patience, and build in more fallbacks.
