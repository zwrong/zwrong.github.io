It's been a while since I felt the joy of learning. It started with reading a blog post and sharing my takeaways with a classmate, then I got some responses and, driven by curiosity, dug deeper and deeper into the underlying principles.

The video was recorded with [Cap](https://github.com/CapSoftware/Cap), and I spent a fair amount of time on the subtitles and the progress bar. The subtitles were generated by using Gemini 3.6 Flash's API (free) to recognize the audio, but the resulting timestamps weren't quite right, and technical terms like Grok and Reasoning Effort needed to be processed again with a larger model (I used Deepseek-v4-flash-Official). The subtitles recognized by Jianying had perfectly aligned timestamps — thanks to [@肖恩君Sean](https://x.com/ShenSeanChen)'s guidance, who told me in the comments that the subtitles were made with Jianying — and combined with the earlier Gemini transcripts, a bit of tweaking got them usable. I finally cracked the subtitle problem.

Burning in the subtitles was done with [Pi](https://pi.dev) paired with the Deepseek-v4-flash-Official model using the FFmpeg CLI. But the first burn produced videos that were not only larger in size but also less sharp than [Cap](https://github.com/CapSoftware/Cap)'s exports. I pasted [Cap](https://github.com/CapSoftware/Cap)'s GitHub repo to the Agent, and it found the highest-quality export settings. In the end the exported video matched [Cap](https://github.com/CapSoftware/Cap)'s exports in both quality and file size.

The progress bar was inspired by [AI半山博士](https://xhslink.cn/m/3KavUUf8xu2)'s GitHub project [chapter-progress-bar-plus](https://github.com/DrBanshan/chapter-progress-bar-plus) and [@张小珺商业访谈录](https://x.com/zhang_benita)'s progress bar style. After a few rounds of tweaking with Deepseek, I got the final look.

The thumbnail was inspired by [@肖恩君Sean](https://x.com/ShenSeanChen)'s cover design. I took a screenshot of the video in PPT, added a white transparent background, and reserved the bottom-right area for the headshot. Finally, I had Grok wrap the bottom-right headshot with the white transparent background.

Overall, AI can help with the subtitles, the progress bar, and the whole process of burning everything into the video. [Pi](https://pi.dev) paired with Deepseek-v4-flash-Official meets my needs. Thumbs up to [Pi](https://pi.dev) and Deepseek!

Thanks to everyone who responded and discussed with me — you gave me the motivation to keep exploring.
